  at half a trading day (T=0.002) the barrier's gamma is 19x the vanilla's and its delta just below H is -13.7
  Delta turns negative below the barrier: near H, a rise in the stock brings the option
  closer to dying. Gamma near H is large and negative and grows like 1/T, because the
  price has to fall from ~$20 of intrinsic value to 0 across a distance ~ sigma sqrt(T);
  a vanilla call's gamma grows only like 1/sqrt(T). A hedger just below H half a day out is
  short ~14 shares per option, and must buy them all back the instant it knocks out.
