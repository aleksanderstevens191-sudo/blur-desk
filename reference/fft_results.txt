16 rows per case: calls and puts, strikes 74 to 135, S0 = 100 at the middle node.
The FFT blurs each row minus its a + b S part (fft.js). Pass: |fft - direct| < 1e-4 near the

on nodes worth up to S0 and < 1e-6 of the value on deep in-the-money nodes worth more ('scaled').
