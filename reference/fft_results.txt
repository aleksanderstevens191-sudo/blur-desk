Accuracy on the Mac: every sweep case within 1e-4 near the money (max 5.3e-5). The Merton case missed:
2.3e-4. Emulating the GPU's f32 arithmetic on the CPU put the error on the direct side: its running
sum of 1197 block sums drifts 1.85e-4 at 38289 taps, while the FFT is off 5.6e-5. The direct shader
now sums blocks of 32 in super-blocks of 1024 (DIRECT_WGSL in gpu-fft.js): 3.2e-5 emulated. Confirmed
on the Mac with the fixed shader (the 38289-tap case now passes at 4.58e-5):
